About This Collection: The Philippines Quezon City North Mission Podcast Archive 2020

Archival Mission & Purpose

Preserving Mission Teachings & Leadership Messages

The Philippines Quezon City North Mission Podcast Archive organizes a collection of over 60 audio recordings preserved from 2020. Created primarily for returned missionaries who served under the leadership of President and Sister Hughes, this digital repository preserves inspiring spiritual guidance, teachings, and missionary advice to encourage lifelong spiritual growth and reflection.
