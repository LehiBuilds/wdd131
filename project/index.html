Episode 2: Act and Not Be Acted Upon, Gratitude as the Highest Agency
On a Sabbath morning amid the escalating disruptions of the early pandemic, the mission president teaches the doctrine of agency, contrasting a proactive mindset with a passive, "cause and effect" mentality. Drawing from 2 Nephi 2 and 11, he illustrates why opposition is structurally essential for spiritual growth. He identifies consistent, pre-emptive gratitude as the most effective vehicle for exercising agency, citing promises from the Doctrine and Covenants and Alma to show that a thankful state naturally invites continuous blessings even during heavy trials.
