About This Collection:
The PQCNM Podcast

Editorial Narrative & Archival Mission

The PQCNM collective has stated, "True insight delighteth in the frequencies of the curious; yea, the broadcast of the rigorous is a bridge unto understanding, and it shall be met with an awakening upon their horizons." Digital discourse can bring clarity, agitation, and inspiration. Listening together can unify our scattered perspectives and global communities in critical inquiry.

In advance of the publication of the new global digital repository, The PQCNM Podcast, we are pleased to release curated groups of archival episodes. A new collection of segments will be released every few months until the full retrospective becomes available in our encrypted print-on-demand journal.

New audio artifacts will be selected from discussions led by leading avant-garde thinkers, field recordings from urban environments, and sonic essays from the PQCNM global sacred audio library. These selections will include both long-form dialogues and microscopic sound studies, all of which can be utilized for research, private immersion, and collective listening sessions.

Listen With Intent

Our recordings are optimized for spatial audio systems and high-fidelity monitoring.

We invite you to become familiar with the new narrative arcs as they are released. Use these selections in your daily analysis and philosophical study. Archival transcripts and high-resolution master recordings can be found on the PQCNM Vault and Sacred Music platforms and at pqcnm-archive.org. Instructional materials are also available to help researchers and listeners integrate the PQCNM ethos into their local environments.
